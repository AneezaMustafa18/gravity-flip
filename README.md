# 🌌 Gravity Flip

### Flip Gravity. Defy Limits. Explore the Unknown. 🚀

**Gravity Flip** is a 2D cosmic adventure game built with **Flutter and Flame Engine**. Explore challenging levels, reverse gravity, dodge obstacles, collect stars, and reach the portal to complete your mission!

A game development project focused on creativity, interactive gameplay, animations, and exploring Flutter beyond traditional mobile and web applications.

---

## 🎮 Game Features

- 🌌 **20 Challenging Levels** – Progress through increasingly challenging stages.
- 🔄 **Gravity Flip Mechanics** – Reverse gravity to navigate the game world.
- ⭐ **Star Collection** – Collect stars and increase your score.
- ❤️ **Three-Life System** – Keep track of your remaining lives.
- 🤖 **Multiple Characters** – Choose from Pip, Nova, Bibi, and Bolt.
- 🚧 **Dynamic Obstacles** – Avoid hazards and navigate challenging platforms.
- 🚪 **Portal-Based Progression** – Reach the portal to complete each level.
- 🎵 **Audio Experience** – Background music and sound effects with music controls.
- 💾 **Progress Saving** – Save unlocked level progress and preferences.
- 📱 **Responsive Gameplay** – Designed for web and mobile screen sizes.

| Technology | Purpose |

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Select your preferred character.
2. Choose an unlocked level.
3. Use the movement controls to navigate platforms.
4. Flip gravity to avoid obstacles and reach difficult areas.
5. Collect stars along your journey.
6. Reach the portal to complete the level.
7. Keep progressing through all 20 levels!

**Your mission:** Master gravity, collect stars, and conquer the cosmic challenges!
